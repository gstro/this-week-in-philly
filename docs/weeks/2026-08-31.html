⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Arlo Parks - Desire Tour
Arlo Parks brings her Ambiguous Desire tour to Union Transfer for a rare Philly headline stop from the Mercury Prize-winning UK artist, touring the nocturnal, dancefloor-inflected material off her third album. It's the kind of visit that's genuinely easy to miss on a quiet Monday and would be talked about after. Union Transfer sits in Callowhill just north of Center City — Broad Street Line to Spring Garden station is a five-minute walk; doors 7, show 8.
Union Transfer, Philadelphia, PA
8:00 PM · Not listed
2
Broken Cameras Screening
Philly DSA and Solidarity Cinema present 5 Broken Cameras, the Oscar-nominated, Sundance-winning documentary shot over five years by a Palestinian farmer defending his village from settlement expansion. It's free programming with real political stakes, paired with a screening space built for conversation rather than passive watching. Suggested donation $6–12; South Street, easy walk from Point Breeze — heads up there's a flight of stairs to the main room.
Nikki Lopez, Philadelphia, PA
7:30 PM · Not listed
3
In-person! West Philadelphia Sanctuary Clay Art-making workshop
West Philadelphia Sanctuary hosts a free, no-experience-necessary clay art-making session at The Rotunda — a low-key communal drop-in built explicitly to give the West Philly community a judgment-free shared space, not to sell a class. University City; the Rotunda sits right off the 34/40 trolley and the Market-Frankford Line's 40th St station.
The Rotunda, 4014 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
7:00 PM · Not listed

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Remembering the Children of Gaza
The Gaza Children's Shroud — a global memorial where mourners hand-write the names of Gaza's more than 20,000 killed children onto a communal shroud — launches its year-long tour of witness right here in Philadelphia before heading to the UN in New York and then Washington. It's a five-hour community signing (1–6 PM) organized by an interfaith coalition, not a passive rally — you can add a name yourself. City Hall, steps from the Broad Street Line.
Philadelphia City Hall, Philadelphia, PA
1:00 PM · Free
2
Pharmakon, Relay For Death, Jacob Winans, $5000, Patrick Gallagher
Pharmakon (Margaret Chardiet) headlines a stacked underground noise/industrial bill with Relay For Death, Jacob Winans, $5000, and Patrick Gallagher — the kind of harsh, confrontational lineup that barely surfaces on mainstream listings. This is the DIY noise scene at its most uncompromising, in a small room built for exactly that. $19.49, 21+, South Street — an easy walk from Point Breeze.
Nikki Lopez, Philadelphia, PA
7:00 PM · $19.49
3
Quicksand + Bane
Quicksand — post-hardcore forebears who bridged Revelation Records-era hardcore with '90s alt-rock — tour with Bane, straight-edge hardcore heavyweights, for a genuinely heavy double bill at Underground Arts. Both carry real weight in hardcore history rather than trading on nostalgia. Callowhill, just north of Center City — Broad Street Line to Spring Garden is the easy route in. $39.30–$45, 21+, doors 6:30.
Underground Arts, Philadelphia, PA
7:30 PM · $39.30-$45, 21+

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
'Fort Bragg Cartel' Reading and Q&A with author Seth Harp
Philadelphia DSA hosts investigative reporter Seth Harp for a reading and Q&A on The Fort Bragg Cartel, his exposé of narcotics trafficking, institutional cover-ups, and unsolved deaths tied to elite U.S. Army special-ops units. Harp is a war veteran turned journalist, so the reporting carries firsthand institutional knowledge rather than outside speculation. Free, Workers United hall on South 22nd Street, Center City — RSVP via Action Network.
Workers United, Philadelphia, PA
4:30 PM · Not listed
2
NLG Know Your Rights Training
The National Lawyers Guild runs a Know Your Rights training at Wooden Shoe Books — practical legal grounding on interacting with police and ICE at a moment when that knowledge is in real demand, not abstract civics. Wooden Shoe is Philly's anchor anarchist bookstore, and this is exactly the political-education programming it exists for. Free, South Street, easy walk from Point Breeze.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:00 PM · Not listed
3
Dekalog: Parts 7 & 8
Kieślowski's Dekalog continues with Parts 7 and 8 — a custody kidnapping and a wrenching Holocaust-era ethics-class confrontation — the latest chapters in the Philadelphia Film Society's run through one of cinema's great achievements. New installments each week keep this fresh even for readers already following along. 4K restoration, Film Society Center, Center City.
Philadelphia Film Society
7:00 PM · Not listed

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Amores Perros
Alejandro González Iñárritu's blistering feature debut gets a new 4K restoration as part of the Philadelphia Film Society's Director Series on him — three interlocking Mexico City stories collided by a car crash, still one of the most kinetic pieces of filmmaking of its era. Paired with a second Iñárritu work in the series, this is a rare chance to see his early work restored on a big screen rather than streamed. Film Society Center, Chestnut Street, Center City.
Philadelphia Film Society
7:00 PM · Not listed
2
GYPSY 83: THE DIRECTOR’S CUT
Todd Stephens' 2001 goth road-trip cult classic gets a genuine restoration event: a new 4K transfer from the original negative, re-edited with over 40 minutes of previously unseen footage and additional goth soundtrack cues, expanding it from 95 to 138 minutes. PhilaMOCA is exactly the room for this — the city's home base for weird, underground, and gothic film culture. Callowhill, just north of Center City; doors 7, film 7:30.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:30 PM · $12
3
The Beyonce Eras Experience
A community-run, costume-encouraged night of Beyoncé's full catalog, from Dangerously in Love through Cowboy Carter, built as a fundraiser for Philly's Black queer community. It opens with a physical-media swap — vinyl and CDs from her catalog as prizes — so there's a record-collecting hook alongside the dance party. Pentridge Station, Kingsessing — a bus ride or BSL-to-bus transfer from Point Breeze, not walkable.
Pentridge Station, Philadelphia, PA
5:00 PM · Not listed
