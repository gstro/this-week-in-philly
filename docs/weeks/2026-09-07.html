⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Naming the Moment: Revolutionary Strategy in a Time of Crisis
A discussion of the 2026 organizing pamphlet "Naming the Moment: Revolutionary Strategy in a Time of Crisis," with project participant Matthew N. Lyons unpacking how an ad hoc group of radical organizers is thinking through resistance to the current administration. It's the kind of political-education night Wooden Shoe does better than almost anywhere else in the city — small room, serious conversation, free to attend.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
7:00 PM · Not listed
2
ANIMATED VIOLENCE (Cali), EARLY GRAVE, KROT (FIRST SHOW)
A stacked hardcore bill at a DIY spot that doesn't show up on the usual aggregators — Animated Violence, Early Grave, and Krot trading sets in what's billed as the first of two Courtyard shows this week. This is the unglamorous, ears-ringing end of the punk spectrum that's easy to miss if you're not already plugged into the scene. *(confirm details — exact neighborhood and full address aren't given by the listing; check the venue's socials before heading out.)*

Peter Hook & The Light
Joy Division and New Order's original bassist has spent over a decade touring his old bands' catalog album by album, and this stop lands on the Substance-era material. It's about as close as anyone gets to hearing that low end played by the person who wrote it — post-punk history, not a tribute act.
Union Transfer, 1026 Spring Garden St., Philadelphia, PA 19123

Tuesday September 8

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Social Distortion
Social Distortion headlines a genuinely stacked punk bill with Descendents and The Chats — three generations of foundational and current punk in one room. Mike Ness and company rarely swing through on a night like this without an arena price tag attached. North Broad Street; the Broad Street Line's Girard Ave stop is a few blocks' walk.
The Met, 858 N Broad St, Philadelphia, PA 19130
7:00 PM · Not listed
2
Save The Academy of Natural Sciences Museum Rally
Philly Science Action and Green Philly are rallying to pressure Drexel into keeping the 200-year-old Academy of Natural Sciences open past this September — a genuinely time-sensitive fight to save an institution, not a recurring protest. On the Ben Franklin Parkway, about a 15-minute walk from Center City or reachable via the 32/38 bus.
The Academy of Natural Sciences of Drexel University, 1900 Benjamin Franklin Pkwy, Philadelphia, PA 19103, USA
10:00 AM · Not listed
3
Nice Strong Arm
Nice Strong Arm were a foundational NYC noise-rock band in the '80s underground, and this is a rare reunion date alongside Shadow Riot and Heavenly Bodies. If you like your rock abrasive and pedigreed, this is the show. Fishtown, at Frankford and Girard — a half-mile walk from the MFL's Girard station, or take the 15 trolley.
Johnny Brenda’s
8:00 PM · $22.90

Wednesday September 9

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
My Friend, Ithamar
A companion piece to last year's The Watcher Sleeps, this Fringe show frames an intimate author reading as a slow-building horror ritual about grief and being watched — three stories that curdle a cozy book event into something unsettling. Small room, single night, exactly the crossover of horror and literature worth seeking out.
Asian Arts Initiative Project Space
9:00 PM · $25
2
Mikey and Nicky
Lightbox Film Center screens a new restoration of Elaine May's 1976 Mikey and Nicky, with Peter Falk and John Cassavetes improvising their way through one very bad night for a low-level mobster. It's one of the great underseen American films of the '70s, and this is a rare chance to see it on a real screen. Right in Point Breeze, at the Bok Building.
Bok Auditorium, 800 Mifflin St, Philadelphia, PA 19148, USA
7:00 PM · Not listed
3
Gay Cum Daddies (Denton) / Sweepers / Good Pollution / Gr3yboy
An experimental/noise-rock/punk bill at a West Philly house venue — four bands built around feedback and dissonance rather than hooks. This is the kind of show that only exists because someone's willing to run a PA out of their living room. Baltimore Ave near 42nd St, in the Cedar Park corridor near University City.
The Pouch (4215 Baltimore Ave Philadelphia)
7:00 PM · Not listed

A hands-on training in de-escalation practices for keeping each other safe at protests and in community spaces, run by the Red Rabbis every second Friday. This is skills-building, not a rally — practical organizing infrastructure that rarely gets its own event listing. Masks required and provided.

Sunday September 13

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Hammered Hulls
A DC hardcore supergroup built from members of Dag Nasty, Girls Against Boys, and Government Issue, playing the kind of tight, riff-forward hardcore that made that scene matter in the first place. In Northern Liberties; the MFL's Spring Garden or Girard station is about a half-mile walk, or take the 57 bus.
Ortlieb's, 847 N 3rd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:00 PM · $21.63, 21+
2
Blank Placard Dance, Replay
A restaging of Anna Halprin's 1967 Blank Placard Dance, in which a silent procession carries empty protest signs through the city and turns the question of what to protest back on the passersby who ask. It's a genuinely strange, pointed piece of protest art performed as a march that starts at FringeArts and moves through Center City — free, and about the closest thing the anti-war art movement of the '60s has left to living history.
FringeArts, 140 N Christopher Columbus Blvd, Philadelphia, PA 19106
11:00 AM · Not listed
3
Film Screening of The Mauritanian, Commemorating 25 Years of the War of Terror
A free screening of The Mauritanian, adapted from Mohamedou Ould Slahi's memoir of 14 years of extrajudicial detention and torture at Guantánamo, timed to the 25th anniversary of the War on Terror. Organizers are framing this explicitly as commemoration and critique, not just a movie night — exactly the political specificity Wooden Shoe does well.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:30 PM · Not listed
