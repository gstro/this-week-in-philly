In-person! Fringe Festival! Ginsburg’s Howl, as read by James Dalton with the Ginsburg Beat Connection
Ginsberg's Howl, read by James Dalton backed by the Ginsberg Beat Connection's revolving Philly/NY/NJ ensemble, sets the poem to jazz, blues, and noise to mark the 100th anniversary of Allen Ginsberg's birth. It's part of this year's Fringe Festival at the Rotunda, the West Philly arts space with the city's strongest track record for free/PWYW literary and experimental programming. $15.
The Rotunda, 4014 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
7:00 PM · Not listed
2
CICADA: I WAS A TEENAGE HORROR MOVIE (1997) + THE VIOLENCE MOVIE (1988)
A double feature of two obscure horror oddities — I Was a Teenage Horror Movie (1997) at 7:00, followed by The Violence Movie (1988) at 7:30 — with the filmmakers in the room for Q&A afterward. PhilaMOCA's repertory horror programming is the city's best for exactly this kind of deep-cut, and a filmmaker visit is what makes this instance worth catching over a normal rep screening. $12.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:00 PM · $12
3
Trans Rights and Bodily Autonomy PowerPoint Party
A PowerPoint-party format built around trans rights and bodily autonomy — a lighthearted structure (short slide decks, wide participation) wrapped around real political stakes. It's participatory rather than a passive rally, and Nikki Lopez has become one of the city's steadier small rooms for this kind of organizing. Free, RSVP via the event's Action Network page.
Nikki Lopez, 304 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
8:00 PM · Not listed

Nai Palm (of Hiatus Kaiyote)
Naomi Saalfield — Nai Palm, the three-time Grammy-nominated voice behind Hiatus Kaiyote — plays a rare solo-leaning date drawing from her intuitive, jazz-soaked songwriting. Ardmore Music Hall is a Main Line venue about 8 miles out; the Paoli/Thorndale Regional Rail line runs straight to Ardmore station, a short walk from the door. 21+, $30–$59.

Free Renters Rights Clinic
A free one-on-one tenant rights clinic — Renters United Philadelphia pairs attendees with a lawyer for real legal advice and walks through know-your-rights training before opening the floor to organizing. ⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Bad Activist
Mai Khôi — once dubbed Vietnam's “Lady Gaga” — left pop stardom to become an internationally recognized human rights activist, including a stint in Hanoi police detention and the Václav Havel Prize for Creative Dissent. Bad Activist is her own telling of that arc: a cabaret-style multimedia autobiography with live music, projections, and archival footage, not a sanitized talk. $15.
Practice Gallery
7:30 PM · $15
2
Birdman
The Philadelphia Film Society's director series on Alejandro González Iñárritu — one of this house's named film anchors — closes with Birdman, Iñárritu's loud, meta backstage comedy about a washed-up superhero actor staging a Raymond Carver play. Michael Keaton, Zach Galifianakis, and Edward Norton co-star.
Philadelphia Film Society
7:00 PM · Not listed
3
Seated Stick Fighting
Strange Fox Fighting Arts teaches a seated adaptation of bataireacht (Irish stick/shillelagh fighting) built specifically for people who can't stand at all or for long — disability justice framed as genuine self-defense instruction, not a token accommodation. Trans folks are prioritized to the front and welcome throughout; bring a cane if you have one, sticks are provided. Free, at Wooden Shoe Books, the anarchist bookstore that consistently runs this kind of accessible, community-first programming.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
7:00 PM · Not listed

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Bikini Kill
Bikini Kill — the Olympia, WA band that effectively started the Riot Grrrl movement, Kathleen Hanna's confrontational lyrics and zine-driven politics setting the template for a generation of feminist punk — reunite for a rare tour stop. This is as close as the current touring circuit gets to the actual originators of the sound, not a tribute or a revival act. Sold out as of this writing; worth checking the venue for a resale or day-of list.
Union Transfer, 1026 Spring Garden St., Philadelphia, PA 19123
8:00 PM · Not listed
2
Historic Drama “These Shining Lives” Opens at The Stagecrafters Theater
These Shining Lives dramatizes the real story of the “Radium Girls” — women at the Radium Dial Company in the 1920s–30s who were poisoned by the glow-in-the-dark paint they were told was safe, and who fought the company for recognition and justice. It's the opening night of the Stagecrafters' 97th season. Chestnut Hill; reachable via SEPTA's Chestnut Hill East or West Regional Rail lines.
Chestnut Hill, Philadelphia, PA
7:30 PM · Not listed
3
“Say it Loud, I’m Black and I’m Proud”
Citizen artist vanessa german transforms two rooms of this historic Germantown rowhouse-turned-museum into an immersive black-box installation for the museum's Semiquincentennial-tied Act Two — sculptural work, soundscape, and site-specific staging built around the idea of “the ordinary colored girl.” Germantown; reachable via SEPTA's Chestnut Hill East Regional Rail or the 23 bus. $20.
The Colored Girls Museum, 4613 Newhall St, Philadelphia, PA 19144
6:00 PM · $20
