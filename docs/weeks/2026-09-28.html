Nobuhiko Obayashi’s SCHOOL IN THE CROSSHAIRS (1981)

Wednesday September 30

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Don't Torture a Duckling
Lucio Fulci's 1972 giallo "Don't Torture a Duckling" — a brutal, hallucinatory murder mystery set in a superstition-soaked southern Italian village — gets a rare theatrical outing at the Film Society's Chestnut Street screen. It's the kind of formative Italian horror that shaped decades of the genre and almost never plays outside a repertory print. Center City, easy to reach.
PFS Film Society Center, 1412 Chestnut Street, Philadelphia, PA 19102
7:30 PM · Not listed
2
Affordable Philly Hearing
Philadelphia City Council hears public testimony on Councilmember O'Rourke's push to make the city more affordable, with tenants from Neighborhood Restorations properties speaking directly to the need for affordable housing. Free and open to the public — a chance to watch housing policy get shaped in real time rather than read about it after the fact. City Hall, Center City.
Philadelphia City Hall, 1400 John F Kennedy Blvd, Philadelphia, PA 19107
10:00 AM · Free
3
Opening Night Of The Surekill Comedy Festival With Ryan Singer
Ryan Singer headlines opening night of the SureKill Comedy Festival — Philadelphia's first paranormal- and horror-themed comedy festival — with horror-leaning comedians from around the country. The Callback Bar hosts pre-show "paranormal festivities" before doors. A genuinely new format for the city's horror calendar, not another haunted house or screening. Punch Line is in Northern Liberties, a short walk from the Market-Frankford El's Girard stop.
Punch Line, 33 E Laurel St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:30 PM · 18+

DEADLY FRIEND (1986)
PhilaMOCA screens Wes Craven's underseen 1986 cult oddity DEADLY FRIEND, about a teenage inventor who reanimates his dead friend with a robot's AI chip — equal parts absurd and unsettling. Two showtimes, 7:00 and 7:30 PM. In Callowhill, a few blocks from the Broad-Ridge Spur's Spring Garden stop.

Historic Drama “These Shining Lives” Opens at The Stagecrafters Theater
The Stagecrafters open "These Shining Lives," dramatizing the real Radium Girls: four dial-factory workers who developed radium poisoning from painting watch faces in the 1920s, and who took their employer to court over it. Community theater staging a genuine labor-rights landmark, opening night. In Chestnut Hill — Regional Rail to Chestnut Hill West or East gets you there from Center City.

Ted Leo and the Pharmacists

On the Main Line: SEPTA's Paoli/Thorndale Regional Rail to Ardmore station, about a 10-minute walk from the platform.

Book Launch for The Pomegranate is a Grenade
Wooden Shoe Books hosts the launch for The Pomegranate is a Grenade by Arab and Muslim writer Maha Hashwi — exactly the kind of politically engaged literary event this anarchist bookstore does best. Union Transfer, 1026 Spring Garden St., Philadelphia, PA 19123
