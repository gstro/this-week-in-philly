Philadelphia Psychotronic Film Society
PhilaMOCA's monthly Psychotronic Film Society meetup is dedicated to the deliberately weird end of genre cinema — exploitation, horror ephemera, and whatever else doesn't fit a normal repertory slot. This screening also closes voting for the 2026 Psychotronic Awards, so it's a bigger night than the usual meetup. Doors 7:00 PM, screening 7:30; Callowhill, a couple of blocks from the Broad Street Line's Spring Garden stop.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:30 PM · $5 At Door
2
Soup Can Magazine Art + Poetry Pop-Up
Soup Can Magazine's roving art-and-poetry pop-up lands at Tattooed Mom with a lineup of Philly writers (Shannon Frost Greenstein among them) — free, no cover, no RSVP required. Tattooed Mom's live-music and performance shows are on hold under an ongoing city permit dispute, but non-musical nights like this one are explicitly still running, so it's a real option, not a maybe.
Tattooed Moms, 530 South Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
7:00 PM · Free
3
DOMi & JD BECK - WHO ASKED? Tour
DOMi & JD Beck are jazz-fusion prodigies barely into their twenties, touring behind the follow-up to their Blue Note debut — a genre-scrambling, virtuosic set that name-checks Herbie Hancock as readily as hyperpop. All ages, standing-room general admission. Fishtown/Olde Richmond, about a 15-minute walk from the Market-Frankford Line's Girard stop, or the 15 trolley to Frankford & Delaware right across the street.
Brooklyn Bowl Philadelphia, 1009 Canal St, Philadelphia, PA 19123

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Militarie Gun
Militarie Gun channel hardcore's speed and DIY ethic through unpolished, almost pop hooks and Ian Shelton's wry, anti-scene-politics lyrics — a genuinely vital band in a genre that's mostly nostalgia acts right now. 21+, $20. Doors 6:00 PM, show 7:00; Callowhill, about two blocks from the Broad Street Line's Spring Garden stop.
Underground Arts, 1200 Callowhill St, Philadelphia, PA 19107
7:00 PM · $20, 21+
2
Marx in Soho by Howard Zinn Featuring Bob Weick
Howard Zinn's one-man play imagines Karl Marx bargaining his way back from the afterlife to defend himself against 2026 America, delivered by longtime interpreter Bob Weick, who's performed the role hundreds of times worldwide. Sharp, funny, and unapologetically didactic — “the words the billionaire class doesn't want you to hear.” $20; opens tonight and runs most of the week. (No confirmed street address for Broken Mirror Studio — sources disagree between a Broad Street and a North 9th Street location, so confirm before heading out.)
Broken Mirror Studio
7:00 PM · $20
3
Body As an Archive
A former Cirque du Soleil performer's autobiographical solo show tracing a childhood inside a brutal military-style circus training system, from age four to a premiere at nine — framed as reclaiming a body and voice that discipline tried to own. Opens tonight, running three nights. $25; Olde Kensington, about a 10-minute walk from the Market-Frankford Line's Girard stop.
Icebox Project Space Gallery
5:30 PM · $25

⭐ Top 3 Picks
1
Minyo Crusaders / Mariposas Galácticas
Minyo Crusaders reinvent Japanese min'yō (traditional folk song) as cumbia, ska, and tropical dance music — bandleader Katsumi Tanaka started the project after the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami sent him digging into folk songs he'd ignored his whole life. Their first-ever North American tour, with Mariposas Galácticas opening. Callowhill, a couple of blocks from the Broad Street Line's Spring Garden stop.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N. 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
8:00 PM · $24.20
2
Radical Ecopoetry to Save the Osprey
Poet Hillary Gonzalez reads from their new collection “Where the Osprey Nest” (Palewell Press) — meditations on ospreys, herons, grief, and the idea that we're not separate from nature but part of it — followed by a Q&A and a briefing on the campaign to save the declining East Coast osprey population. Classic Wooden Shoe programming: politically engaged literature with a concrete cause attached.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:30 PM · Not listed
3
In-person! Secret Cinema pres. D for Design: Form and Function at Mid-Century. films on 16mm
Secret Cinema's rare-16mm-print series turns its projector on mid-century design films for this one — “Form and Function at Mid-Century,” part of an ongoing archive-diving project distinct from the Rotunda's standing Wednesday jam session happening down the hall. Free/PWYW, University City.
The Rotunda, 4014 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

Fabio Frizzi performs FRIZZI2FULCI
Fabio Frizzi — the composer behind Lucio Fulci's gore classics *Zombi 2*, *The Beyond*, and *City of the Living Dead* — performs those scores live on his first North American tour in support of them. This is about as direct a hit on PhilaMOCA's whole reason for existing as a booking gets: Italian horror, live, at the venue built for exactly this. The Elements of Mutual Aid

SEX PISTOLS (Steve Jones, Paul Cook, Glen Matlock) feat. Frank Carter

Ray Cappo talks 90’s Krishnacore

Take Your Shot: A Motivational Seminar with Luigi Mangione
Satirical immersive theater staged as a mandatory corporate motivational seminar — fictional “Ramco Laboratories” brings in Luigi Mangione (framed here as a corporate-America folk antihero, not the real defendant in an active criminal case) to inspire employees to “take your shot.” Pointed, uncomfortable, and exactly the kind of anti-corporate satire that doesn't get made by anyone with something to lose. Chinatown North/Callowhill, close to the Broad Street Line's Race-Vine or Spring Garden stops.
Asian Arts Initiative Black Box Theater
2:00 PM · $25
3
Moral Injury
Two castaways attempt to hunt and eat a pig with predictably dire results — a genre-blending piece built on heavy movement, mask, and sound work that uses survival-horror absurdity to get at something more serious about trauma and complicity. Fringe programming at First Unitarian Church, better known for its punk shows.
First Unitarian Church of Philadelphia
2:00 PM · $20
