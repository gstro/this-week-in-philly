Monday October 19

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Socialist Night School: Abolition 101
Philly DSA's Socialist Night School turns to abolition this week, tracing the fight to defund police and prisons here back to global movements against racial capitalism and imperialism. It's political education in the true sense — an internationalist abolitionist framework, not just local talking points. Free, at Wooden Shoe, the city's anarchist bookstore and the most reliable venue in town for exactly this kind of programming.
Wooden Shoe
6:30 PM · Not listed
2
Special Event | Rare Finds: A Conversation With Collector Edward Leahy | In-Person
Collector Edward Leahy opens up eight centuries of literary history for one intimate evening at the Rosenbach — illuminated manuscripts, jeweled bindings, incunabula, and what's likely the world's finest private Beatrix Potter collection, alongside first editions of horror classics. A rare chance to hear a serious collector talk through how and why he built this, not just look at it behind glass.
The Rosenbach Museum and Library, 2008-2010 Delancey St
5:00 PM · Not listed
3
Mike D 5D
Mike Diamond — Mike D of the Beastie Boys — brings his “5D” tour to Union Transfer, a rare non-NYC stop from a founding figure of New York hip-hop who discovered punk rock as a teenager and never fully let go of it. Expect stories as much as a set: this reads as a conversational, career-spanning night rather than a straight concert. Spring Garden, a few blocks from the Broad Street Line's Spring Garden stop.
Union Transfer, 1026 Spring Garden St., Philadelphia, PA 19123
8:00 PM · Not listed

Claudio Simonetti’s Goblin
Claudio Simonetti's Goblin perform their score live, synced to a screening of Dario Argento's Tenebre (1982) — then return after the film for a second set of other Goblin material, from a composer whose other scores (Suspiria, Dawn of the Dead, Deep Red, Demons) shaped horror cinema's sound as much as any director shaped its look. It's a trek: the Colonial Theatre is in Phoenixville, about 30 miles northwest of Center City, so plan on a car or a long transit day.

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
L7: The Last Hurrah Tour 2026
L7 bring their “Last Hurrah” farewell tour to Brooklyn Bowl — a legitimately rare chance to see one of the loudest, funniest bands of the grunge/riot-grrrl crossover before they're off the road for good. Elfreth’s Alley Museum, 126 Elfreth's Alley, Philadelphia, PA 19106
