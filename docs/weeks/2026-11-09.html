Daughters of the Dust—with Ashley Clark

Wednesday November 11

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Demographic Anxieties: A Conversation with Gala Rexer
Gala Rexer (University of Warwick) and Nada Matta (Drexel) discuss Rexer's new book on how Israeli state surveillance and border policy shape Palestinian women's reproductive freedom, drawn from two years of ethnographic fieldwork. Dense, serious political-education programming — exactly what Wooden Shoe anchors. Free.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:30 PM · Not listed
2
Eugene Debs: American Socialist
A talk tied to a new book on Eugene Debs — five-time Socialist Party presidential candidate and the most prominent socialist in American history. Rare to see an event built explicitly around Debs rather than invoking him in passing; useful grounding for anyone interested in the real history behind 'democratic socialism.' Free, Rittenhouse Square.
Philadelphia Ethical Society
6:30 PM · Not listed
3
Chat Pile
Chat Pile's lurching sludge-noise headlines over Philly's own Soul Glo, whose ferocious, explicitly political hardcore has made them one of the most vital bands the city's produced this decade. Shallowater and Skullpresser round out a stacked bill.
Union Transfer
7:30 PM · Not listed
