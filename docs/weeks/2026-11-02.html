Dirty Old Power Tour | AJJ
AJJ (Andrew Jackson Jihad) bring their Dirty Old Power tour to Philly's most storied punk venue, with Greet Death opening. Folk-punk with teeth and a political streak, all ages, at a church that's hosted hardcore and DIY shows for decades. Doors 7:30, show 8; $33.02.
The First Unitarian Church
8:00 PM · $33.02
2
Palestine Cinema Days 2026: Foragers
Palestine Cinema Days returns with "Foragers," a documentary feature screening at Vox Populi, the artist-run gallery and project space in Chinatown North. A film program with explicit political stakes rather than a passive watch -- exactly the kind of screening easy to miss outside a dedicated series like this.
Vox Populi
7:00 PM · Not listed
3
BIG NIGHT screening & TIMPANO tasting!
Thirty years of "Big Night," Stanley Tucci and Campbell Scott's beloved Jersey Shore food film, celebrated with a screening and three Philly chefs' own takes on the movie's showpiece timpano. A members' press club turning a cult food movie into an actual tasting is the kind of unrepeatable mashup worth building a Monday around.

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
NASH THE SLASH RISES AGAIN!
Nash the Slash -- the mummy-bandaged, gas-masked Canadian avant-garde violinist and glass-harmonica player -- built a cult following over four decades before his death in 2014; this is most likely a tribute night resurrecting his persona and catalog rather than the man himself, but PhilaMOCA is exactly the room built for that stunt. $12, two sets (7:00 and 7:30). In Callowhill just above Chinatown; the Broad Street Line's Spring Garden stop is the closest way in.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:00 PM · $12
2
NLG Know Your Rights and Risks Training
The National Lawyers Guild runs a practical know-your-rights-and-risks training at Wooden Shoe Books -- exactly the kind of civic-defense programming the anarchist bookstore's political-education line is built around, and genuinely useful beyond the evening itself.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:00 PM · Not listed

A. Rimbaud
Lightbox Film Center brings its Philadelphia premiere of "A. Rimbaud" to Bok, pairing the city's premier repertory/arthouse programmer with a film named for the poet -- a literary subject getting a cinephile's treatment rather than a biopic gloss.
Bok Auditorium, 800 Mifflin St, Philadelphia, PA 19148, USA
