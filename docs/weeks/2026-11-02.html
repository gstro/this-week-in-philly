AJJ (formerly Andrew Jackson Jihad) bring their scrappy, politically sharp folk-punk to Philly on the Dirty Old Power tour, with Greet Death opening. 🎶 Learn to Livecode with Strudel!
Iffy Books hosts a beginner-friendly introduction to Strudel, a live-coding tool for making music with code, ending in a group livecode jam. No programming experience required — DIY electronics, functional-style programming, and music overlapping in exactly the way this space does best.

Bow and Arrow (Lea Bertucci + Michael Behari) & Angel Meat (Anna Young + Myles Donovan)
Experimental composer Lea Bertucci (paired with Michael Behari as Bow and Arrow) splits a bill with the duo Angel Meat at The Perch in Kensington — a small-room experimental/noise show that's easy to miss. Bertucci's work spans tape loops, saxophone, and site-specific sound installation; expect something more textural than a standard rock bill. A quick ride up the Market-Frankford Line into Kensington.
The Perch (Large Room)

25 Years of 'The Disintegration Loops' | A Special Seated Show with | William Basinski
William Basinski brings "The Disintegration Loops" — his landmark, elegiac ambient work built from tape loops literally dissolving as they played, recorded in the days around 9/11 — to a seated 25th-anniversary performance in First Unitarian's sanctuary, with claire rousay opening. The DIY-electronics-meets-music crossover this space exists for, and a real rarity on the Philly calendar.
