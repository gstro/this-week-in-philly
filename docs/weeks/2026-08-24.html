DUST DEVIL (1992)
PhilaMOCA screens Dust Devil, Richard Stanley's near-mythic 1992 desert horror about a shapeshifting serial killer stalking the South African wasteland — a film that spent decades in butchered cuts before its restoration made repertory bookings like this one possible. It's the kind of genre deep-cut that made PhilaMOCA's reputation, projected in a former mausoleum showroom instead of a multiplex. Doors 7:00 PM, film at 7:30 PM; $10.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:30 PM · $10

Little Haiti Chapter Meeting
The Little Haiti Chapter hosts a community causerie on immigration and affordable housing, bringing residents, advocates, and local leaders together to compare notes on two of the crises hitting Philadelphia's immigrant communities hardest right now. It's a working conversation rather than a rally — small, substantive, and easy to miss if you're not already plugged into the group. Free.

Rustin's Challenge Reading Group
A discussion group on Bayard Rustin's overlooked "Freedom Budget for All Americans" — the jobs-centric economic vision from the organizer who built the March on Washington but rarely gets credit for the ideas behind it. Led by Paul Prescod, it's political education in the literal sense: reading and arguing through an alternative to austerity politics, not just another lecture. Free, Rittenhouse Square.

📍 Offline GPS Unit Workshop
Build a fully offline GPS unit at Iffy Books — a Pi Pico microcontroller, a GPS module, and a tiny OLED display, wired up in MicroPython to show your coordinates and the time with no internet or cell connection at all. It's DIY electronics at its most quietly radical: a gadget that works precisely because it can't be tracked, monetized, or bricked by a company's server going dark. Requires a ~$40-50 parts kit, purchased separately.
