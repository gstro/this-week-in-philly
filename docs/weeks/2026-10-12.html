Monday October 12

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
*Depose: Luigi Mangione, the Right to Health, and the Unfinished American Revolution* Book Launch Tour
A spokesperson for the anonymous collective behind Luigi Mangione's legal fund presents "Depose," a book arguing his case is really about the right to health care in America. Wooden Shoe is the natural room for this — anarchist bookstore meets explicitly political publishing (Ill Will Editions, distributed by MIT Press), out the next day. Free, South Street, easy walk from Point Breeze.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
7:00 PM · Not listed
2
Sugar
Bob Mould's Sugar reunite to play Copper Blue, the 1992 NME Album of the Year that bridged Hüsker Dü's hardcore roots into melodic alt-rock — essential listening for anyone who traces post-hardcore's lineage. A genuinely rare reunion show at a venue built for it. Tuesday's second Philly night, same tour, is a fallback if this one's sold out by the time you look.
Union Transfer, 1026 Spring Garden St., Philadelphia, PA 19123
8:00 PM · SOLD OUT
3
Selected Work: Walé Oyéjidé
The closing night of HOMEPLACE screens a film by Walé Oyéjidé — an award-winning fashion designer turned filmmaker — followed by a conversation with Àrokò Cooperative's design director. Small sliding-scale art space on Pine Street, Center City; the kind of one-off programming that doesn't repeat.
1803 Pine St, Philadelphia, PA 19103, USA
6:00 PM · Not listed

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
URGH! A MUSIC WAR – Screening + Presentation
The "Professors of Urgh" (Chaki and Chris Slusarenko) bring their touring presentation of URGH! A MUSIC WAR, the 1981 concert film that's basically a time capsule of punk and new wave's first wave — Devo, the Dead Kennedys, Gang of Four, Klaus Nomi. PhilaMOCA is exactly the room for it. Doors 7:00 PM, screening 7:30 PM, $17.
PhilaMOCA, 531 N 12th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7:30 PM · $17
2
Authors Alysia Li Ying Sawchyn & Meghana Mysore
Alysia Li Ying Sawchyn and Meghana Mysore co-launch their new books — I Have This Thing for Flowers and Let All Our Ghosts Depart — at the Head & the Hand, a scrappy independent press and bookstore in Fishtown. Twin debuts, built-in conversation between the two writers. Fishtown, roughly near the Berks stop on the Market-Frankford Line (noting that from general knowledge of the corridor, not the listing itself).
The Head & The Hand Books, 2230 Frankford Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19125
7:00 PM · Not listed

Tuesday Nite Music Club w/ Taylor Kelly
Taylor Kelly — a Philly-scene fixture with a voice rooted in jazz, soul, and funk — plays an intimate early set at 48 Record Bar before the club spins a classic LP start to finish for a listening session. This is exactly the small-room, record-store-adjacent night that's easy to miss. Old City, a few blocks from the 2nd St stop on the Market-Frankford Line.
48 Record Bar, 48 S 2nd St., Philadelphia, PA 19106
7:00 PM · Not listed

⭐ Top 3 Picks

1
Get on the Job and Organize Book Talk
Jaz Brisack, the Rhodes Scholar-turned-barista who co-founded Starbucks Workers United, talks through "Get on the Job and Organize" — a practical account of beating union-busting from the inside. Timely labor organizing at the bookstore built for it. Free, South Street.
Wooden Shoe Books, 704 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19147
6:30 PM · Not listed
2
World to Come 2: Infra Dig
Free, open-air "Creative Music" — the free-jazz/AACM-adjacent tradition Philly claims through Coltrane and Sun Ra — performed in Christ Church's gardens as part of the Collective Futures series. Polymodal improvisation outdoors, no ticket required.
Christ Church Gardens
5:30 PM · Not listed
3
Latinas Writing Horror
Three Latina horror authors — Ann Dávila Cardinal and Diana Rodríguez Wallach among them — read and discuss short horror fiction for Latine Heritage Month at Taller Puertorriqueño, North Philly's long-running Latino cultural arts center. A genuinely specific intersection of horror and literature you won't find on a bigger stage. Norris Square, North Philly; nearest Market-Frankford El stop is roughly Somerset or Huntingdon, about a half-mile off (going by the area's general layout, not a stated transit line).
Taller Puertorriqueño 2600 N 5th St
7:00 PM · Not listed
